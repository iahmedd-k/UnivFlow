# Uniflow Claude Companion (Chrome Extension)

Connect your Claude.ai Free or Pro account to Uniflow in **1 click** with zero VNC latency, zero Google OAuth blocks, and no Cloudflare captchas.

---

## 🚀 Installation in 3 Steps

1. Open Google Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top-right corner).
3. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left button) and select this `extension/` folder:
   ```
   d:\Ahmed Dev\Projects\Uniflow\extension
   ```

---

## ⚡ How to Sync

1. Make sure you are logged into [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) in your Chrome browser.
2. Click the **Uniflow Claude Companion** icon in your Chrome toolbar.
3. Click **⚡ Sync Session to Uniflow** (or click **Copy sessionKey** and paste it into Uniflow Dashboard → Settings).
4. Head to Uniflow Settings and check **"Automatically solve eligible assignments"**.

Done! Uniflow will now automatically solve your assignments in the background using your private Claude session without opening any slow browser tabs.
