# Uniflow Academic Companion (Chrome Extension)

Connect your **Claude.ai**, **Google Classroom**, and **University Portal** accounts to Uniflow in **1 click** with zero VNC latency, zero Google bot blocks, and full autonomous background execution.

---

## 🚀 Installation in 3 Steps

1. Open Google Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top-right corner).
3. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left button) and select this `extension/` folder:
   ```
   d:\Ahmed Dev\Projects\Uniflow\extension
   ```

---

## ⚡ How to Sync

1. Make sure you are logged into:
   - [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) (for AI assignment solving)
   - [classroom.google.com](https://classroom.google.com) (for autonomous background turn-in)
   - [portals.au.edu.pk](https://portals.au.edu.pk/students/) (for attendance & timetable sync)
2. Click the **Uniflow Academic Companion** icon in your Chrome toolbar.
3. Click:
   - **⚡ Sync Claude**: Syncs Claude session for automated solving.
   - **⚡ Sync Classroom**: Syncs Google Classroom session once so Uniflow's background server can turn in assignments automatically before the deadline—even when your laptop is turned off!
   - **⚡ Sync Portal**: Syncs university records & attendance.

Done! Uniflow will now automatically solve and turn in your assignments in the background.
